Pricing Formula

Retail = (Material Cost + Making Charge) × Multiplier + Stone Cost
Material Cost = Weight (g) × Spot (ZAR/g) × 1.02 (premium)
Multiplier = 2.2 (gold) / 2.3 (silver) · Stone markup = +40–80%

You aim for cost plus 30–60% on metal — fair to you, fair to the customer, and still well under mall prices.

Risk & Compliance

Built to Survive Audits

Legal (South Africa)

  • • Register business (CIPC + SARS); confirm VAT threshold & invoicing rules.
  • • Obtain jeweller's permit + JCSA accreditation; register hallmark/maker's mark.
  • • Precious metals compliance; consumer protection (CPA) on returns/warranties.
  • • Insurance (studio, tools, stock, transit); home-based zoning check.
  • • POPIA privacy policy, terms, shipping & returns on the site.

Risk Mitigation

  • • Outsource casting; do NOT buy a casting kit until volume justifies.
  • • No pavé/micro-pavé until microscope + advanced setting training.
  • • No platinum fabrication until dedicated tool set & torch upgrade.
  • • 50% deposit on bespoke; signed commission agreement; 2 revisions max in writing.
  • • Re-quote spot on the day of fabrication; add 3–5% currency buffer.
  • • Heirloom intake: identification/appraisal before quoting; written consent.

FAQ

How long does a bespoke ring take?

Typically 3–6 weeks from approved CAD render, including stone sourcing, fabrication, setting and QC.

Do you work with client-supplied gold?

Yes. Heirloom redesign is a signature service — we melt, refine and re-alloy your gold, with a provenance card documenting origin.

Are your stones certified?

We use GIA/EGL-certified natural diamonds and lab-grown stones, plus moissanite and certified cabochons. Certificates supplied on request.

What is the deposit policy?

50% deposit on approval of the CAD render, balance on delivery. Design fee credited to the final piece.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes — tracked, insured courier. International shipping (DHL/FedEx) available on request.

Ethical & Bespoke

Heritage + Honesty

Your default way of working — handmade, repairable, lasting — IS the sustainable story.

Ethical Sourcing Story

  • Craft as sustainability — handmade, repairable, remodellable; buy once, wear forever; anti-fast-fashion.
  • Provenance — "I know where every gram of gold and every stone came from." Provenance card in every box.
  • Heirloom — pieces designed to outlive trends; family stories embedded in metal.
  • Local — made in South Africa, by a South African hand, supporting local craft.
  • Transparency — show the bench, the suppliers, the certificates. "Ask me anything."
  • Repair & reuse — remodel inherited jewellery; recycle client gold. The most authentic circular claim.

Certification ladder: provenance cards → recycled-metal audit → GIA/EGL stones only → RJC micro-enterprise → Fairmined licensee → RJC CoP. Disclose what is not yet certified.

Bespoke Commission Journey (9 Steps)

  1. Discovery / Lead — IG/FB/WhatsApp/referral; client shares inspiration + budget.
  2. Consultation (free, 45–90 min) — in person or video; story, budget, metal, stones, lifestyle, timeline; inspect heirlooms.
  3. Stone sourcing — present certified options (natural/lab-grown/moissanite); client selects.
  4. Design — hand sketches → CAD render → 3D images; 2 revisions included.
  5. Approval + deposit — approve render, sign commission agreement, 50% deposit.
  6. Fabrication — wax/CAM → casting → setting → finishing; heirloom: melt, refine, re-alloy.
  7. Quality check — setting security, polish, sizing, engraving.
  8. Delivery / presentation — in-person handover or tracked insured courier; reveal moment.
  9. Aftercare — free first resize, cleaning, polishing, setting warranty. Repeat client.

Consultation is the sale. CAD approval is the risk gate. Heirloom intake needs appraisal before quoting. Remote clients via WhatsApp video — standard now.

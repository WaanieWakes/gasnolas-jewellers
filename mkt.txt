## 11. 30-Day Launch Plan (Daily Tasks)

Assume starting from zero. Each day ≈ 60–90 min. Adjust to your capacity.

### Week 1 — Foundations
- **Day 1:** Define brand name, handle, one-line positioning ("49-year SA jeweler — bespoke, heirloom, repair"). Set up WhatsApp Business number + profile.
- **Day 2:** Create Instagram + Facebook pages; write bios; link WhatsApp click-to-chat; set up highlights covers.
- **Day 3:** Create Google Business Profile (SAB, service area). Start verification.
- **Day 4:** Build WhatsApp catalog (first 20 pieces: photos, prices, descriptions).
- **Day 5:** Shoot 15 photos + 5 short videos (process + finished pieces) on phone.
- **Day 6:** Write 30 content captions in a bank; plan hashtag sets.
- **Day 7:** Review GBP verification; ask 5 past clients for Google reviews.

### Week 2 — Content Engine
- **Day 8:** Post first Reel (process) + FB cross-post. Post WhatsApp Status.
- **Day 9:** Post second Reel (heirloom story) + 3 photos. Join 5 FB community groups.
- **Day 10:** Post third Reel (education). Send partner outreach to 5 wedding planners.
- **Day 11:** Post TikTok (first). Reply to all comments/DMs within 1 hour.
- **Day 12:** Post Reel (before/after repair). Send partner kit to 2 salons.
- **Day 13:** Post FB group value post (jewelry care tip). Post WhatsApp Status x2.
- **Day 14:** Weekly review: which post got most saves/shares? Note top format. Plan next week.

### Week 3 — Partnerships & Paid
- **Day 15:** Apply to 2 craft markets for next month.
- **Day 16:** Apply to 1 bridal fair; list on Pink Book / SA Weddings directories.
- **Day 17:** Set up Meta Ads: 1 ad set, R50/day, WhatsApp objective, 3 creatives.
- **Day 18:** Set up Google Ads Search: R100/day, 5 keywords, 3 ad variations.
- **Day 19:** Post Reel (49-years lesson). Outreach to 5 more creators (gifting).
- **Day 20:** Host a mini open-studio (invite 5 past clients + 2 partners).
- **Day 21:** Weekly review: ad spend, leads, cost per lead. Pause losers.

### Week 4 — Scale & Systemize
- **Day 22:** Post best-performing Reel format again (variation). Add 10 more catalog items.
- **Day 23:** Send first WhatsApp broadcast to segmented list (value + new piece).
- **Day 24:** Post TikTok + IG Reel. Pitch 3 corporate gifting prospects (Dec season).
- **Day 25:** Collect 3 video testimonials from happy clients.
- **Day 26:** Refresh GBP: new photos, a Google Post, reply to all reviews.
- **Day 27:** Co-host a trunk show with a bridal boutique.
- **Day 28:** Review month: revenue, leads, top channel, top content, top partner.
- **Day 29:** Decide scale: increase ad budget 20% on winner; book next market.
- **Day 30:** Document SOPs (content batch day, weekly review, partner follow-up). Set 90-day goals.

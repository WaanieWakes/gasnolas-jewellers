## 4. Custom Quote Formula

### Core formula (cost-plus — recommended default)

```
Retail price = (Gold/silver weight (g) × Spot rate for purity (R/g))
             + (Making charge (R/g × weight)  OR  % of metal value)
             + (Stone cost × stone markup)
             + (Setting labour)
             + (Extras: engraving / plating / design fee)
             + (Wastage allowance 2–4% of metal)
             + (Profit margin %)
```

### Worked example — 18k custom ring, 4.2 g, simple solitaire (CZ cost R 120)

| Line | Calc | Amount (ZAR) |
|---|---|---|
| Gold material | 4.2 g × R 1,657.34 | R 6,960.83 |
| Making charge (standard, 18k) | 4.2 g × R 400 | R 1,680.00 |
| Stone | R 120 × 1.6 | R 192.00 |
| Setting labour | 1 stone × R 200 | R 200.00 |
| Consumables / overhead | 5% of (material + making) | R 432.04 |
| Wastage (3% of metal) | 3% × R 6,960.83 | R 208.82 |
| Subtotal cost | | R 9,673.69 |
| Profit margin (35%) | × 1.35 | **R 13,059.48** |
| Round to retail | | **R 13,050** |

### Stone pricing rules
- Cost stones (CZ, synthetic): cost × 1.5 – 2.0.
- Natural diamonds / gemstones: cost × 1.3 – 1.6 (lower %, higher absolute) + certification + setting.
- **Always quote stones separately** from metal so the client sees the split and can swap.

### Weight & wastage
- Quote on **finished weight** plus **2–4% wastage allowance** on metal (filing, polishing, sprue).
- Disclose or absorb into making charge; be consistent.

### Markup strategies

| Strategy | When to use | Typical markup |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-plus | Standard custom, repairs | 30–50% on metal+making; 40–80% on stones |
| Value-based | Bespoke, heirloom, signature designs | 2× – 5× cost |
| Competitive | Plain bands, chains, commodity items | 10–20% below chains |
| Tiered (recommended) | Mix of stock + custom | Stock 20–30%, custom 40–60%, bespoke 60%+ |

- Never price below cost-plus floor. Keep a **minimum 25% margin** even on "favours".
- Re-price quotes if gold moves **> 2%** between quote and acceptance.
- Round retail prices to clean numbers (R 13,050, not R 13,059.48).

### Currency (ZAR volatility)
- Quote validity **48 h** with re-quote clause if gold moves > 2%.
- Add **3–5% currency buffer** to the overhead line.
- Track the rate weekly; adjust the price list monthly.
- Buy per job to avoid holding depreciating metal; buy in larger batches when the rand is strong.

```
Gold price re-quoted if spot moves > 2% between quote and acceptance.
```

**Quoting rules**
- Always quote **weight + purity + spot rate** so the client sees the metal is market-linked.
- Separate metal, making, and stones — never one lump sum.
- Take **50% deposit before buying metal**; metal is non-returnable.
- Note **lead time**; adjust invoice at collection for weight variance.
- Repairs/restoration: flat labour fee + materials (no metal markup if client supplies gold).
- Keep a **written record** (photo of piece + weight + purity) for disputes.

## 6. Payment Terms

- **Currency:** ZAR only (local clients). Foreign/export clients: quote USD-equivalent converted to ZAR at payment.
- **Methods accepted:** EFT (preferred), cash, card (if reader available). No cheques.
- **Invoicing:** itemised invoice per the quotation template; keep records for re-orders and disputes.
- **VAT:** confirm registration threshold and invoicing rules with a SA tax practitioner; state on invoice whether prices are VAT-inclusive or exclusive.
- **Late / overdue:** work not released until balance settled; deposits non-refundable once metal is purchased.
- **Refunds:** material (metal) non-refundable after purchase; labour refundable only if work not yet started.

## 7. Deposit Policy

- **Standard custom / bespoke:** **50% deposit** before material purchase; **50% balance on collection/delivery**.
- **Heirloom redesign:** **50% deposit** (or R 1,000 minimum, whichever is higher) before work begins; balance on delivery. Client's metal weighed and recorded before work.
- **Repairs:** **100% upfront** for jobs under R 1,000; 50% deposit for jobs over R 1,000.
- **Stock / ready-made pieces:** full payment on purchase; lay-by by arrangement (max 60 days, non-refundable deposit).
- **Deposit is non-refundable** once metal is purchased or work has commenced; communicate this clearly in writing.
- **No scope creep:** any change to spec after deposit requires a written re-quote and adjusted deposit.

### Heirloom-specific consent
- Photograph + weigh + test metal (acid/XRF) before work.
- Written consent form; never promise to "keep all metal" unless weighed and agreed.
- Return all unused metal or credit it at agreed rate.
- Deliver with before/after photos.

## 9. Notes for the Maker

- Compete on **craft, customisation, and service** — not on commodity gold price.
- Keep a **cost sheet** per piece (metal weight, spot used, making, stones, margin %, retail) — needed for re-orders and disputes.
- Store metal cost in a spreadsheet: Date, Purity, Weight, Spot R/g, Making R/g, Stone cost, Margin %, Retail.
- Hallmark every piece (metal + maker's mark); register maker's mark via Jewellery Council of SA.
- Avoid: lost-wax casting, pave/micro-pavé, intricate filigree, invisible/tension settings, platinum (unless fully equipped) — outsource instead.
- Highest margin lines: hand-fabricated bands, signets, name pendants, bezel pendants, studs, bangles, chain repair, and **heirloom redesign** (3–5× on labour only).

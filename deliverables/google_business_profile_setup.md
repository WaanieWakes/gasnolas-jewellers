# Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup Guide
## Home-Based Jeweler — South Africa (Service-Area Business)

**Prepared:** October 2026 | **Operator profile:** 49-year veteran bench jeweler, home studio (by appointment), selling bespoke/handcrafted jewelry, repairs, remodeling and valuations in South Africa.
**Goal:** Rank in "jeweler near me", "custom engagement ring [city]", "jewelry repair [city]" searches and convert searchers into WhatsApp enquiries — without publishing the home address.
**Companion research:** `D:/AI_Lab/research/06_marketing_channels.md` (§1 GBP) and `D:/AI_Lab/research/01_local_commerce_sa.md` (§4.3 GBP, §7 legal).

## 1. Create the Profile (Service-Area Business)

1. Go to **business.google.com** → sign in with a Google account you control long-term (not a personal throwaway).
2. Click **"Manage now"** → **"Add your business to Google"**.
3. Enter your **exact business name** (no keyword stuffing — Google suspends profiles that add "jeweler Cape Town" to the name).
4. **Business category:** choose **"Jeweler"** as primary (see §2).
5. **Location type:** select **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** (Service-Area Business). **Do not** enter your home address as a public storefront — Google's SAB guidelines require hiding the address for home-based businesses. You may enter the address privately for verification only.
6. **Service area:** add your city + surrounding suburbs / 25–50 km radius (see §7).
7. **Contact details:** phone (WhatsApp-capable) + website/IG link.
8. **Business hours** + short description (see §3).
9. Finish and **start verification immediately** (§6).

> **Critical:** An unverified profile rarely ranks. Treat verification as a blocking task before any marketing spend.

## 3. NAP (Name, Address, Phone) Consistency

NAP consistency is a local SEO ranking factor. The exact same strings must appear on GBP, Instagram bio, Facebook page, WhatsApp Business profile, website, and every directory.

### NAP standard (fill in and copy verbatim everywhere)

| Field | Your standard value | Where it must match |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | e.g. "Thabo Bespoke Jewellery" (one spelling) | GBP, IG, FB, WhatsApp Business, website footer, email signature, directory listings (Pink Book, SA Weddings, GoAfricaOnline, InfoIsInfo, YelloSA, JCSA). |
| **Address (private)** | Home studio address (entered for verification only; hidden publicly) | Verification postcard/video; never published. Public-facing: "By appointment, [City]". |
| **Address (public-facing)** | "By appointment only — [City], South Africa" | Website, IG bio, WhatsApp Business description, GBP description. |
| **Phone** | +27 XX XXX XXXX (WhatsApp-capable) | GBP, IG bio link, FB, WhatsApp Business, website, all directories. Use one format everywhere: `+27 XX XXX XXXX`. |
| **Email** | business@email.co.za | Website, GBP, directories. |
| **Website/IG** | https://instagram.com/yourhandle or wa.me link | GBP "Website" field. |

### NAP audit checklist
- [ ] Spelling of business name identical across all platforms (decide "Jewellery" vs "Jewelry" once).
- [ ] Phone number identical format (+27…) everywhere.
- [ ] No old numbers/addresses lingering on old directory listings (search your name + "jeweler" and claim/update/remove stale entries).
- [ ] Directories to align: Pink Book, SA Weddings, GoAfricaOnline, InfoIsInfo, YelloSA, JCSA member directory, Brabys, Hotfrog, SA Weddings supplier listings.
- [ ] Google's "Suggest an edit" — monitor and reject incorrect edits from the public.

## 4. Photos Checklist (10+ minimum, add 2–3/week)

Google favours fresh, original photos. Avoid stock images. Aim for 15–25 photos at launch, then 2–3 new per week.

### Launch photo set
| # | Photo | Purpose / tips |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logo (square, high-res) | Profile identity. |
| 2 | Cover photo — hero piece on hand or bench | First impression in search results. |
| 3 | Workshop bench (wide) | Shows craft credibility; well-lit, tidy. |
| 4 | Tools close-up (files, pliers, torch, loupe) | "49 years at the bench" story. |
| 5 | Hands at work (soldering/polishing/setting) | Process content; high engagement. |
| 6 | Before/after repair (side-by-side) | Proof of skill; huge trust builder. |
| 7 | Finished engagement ring (macro) | Hero product for bridal searchers. |
| 8 | Finished piece on hand / worn | Scale and lifestyle context. |
| 9 | Heirloom restoration (before/after) | Emotional, shareable. |
| 10 | Packaging / gift box | Signals professionalism and care. |
| 11 | Valuation certificate sample | Trust for insurance-valuation queries. |
| 12 | Owner portrait at bench | Personal brand; "master craftsman" positioning. |

### Ongoing (weekly)
- [ ] 1 new finished-piece macro (rotating collections: engagement, everyday, heirloom, repairs).
- [ ] 1 process/bench shot (filing, stone setting, polishing, engraving).
- [ ] 1 before/after (repair or remodel) — highest save/share rate.

### Photo rules
- **Format:** JPG/PNG, min 720×720 px (1080×1080 ideal), under 5 MB.
- **No watermarks** with contact details overlaid (Google may reject); logo watermark is fine.
- **Geo-tagging:** shoot on phone with location services on so photos carry your city metadata (helps local ranking).
- **Captions:** add a short description to each photo in GBP (e.g., "Hand-forged 18ct gold engagement ring — Cape Town studio").
- **Avoid:** stock photos, supplier catalog images, photos with other people's faces without consent, blurry/dark shots.

## 6. Verification Steps

Google verifies SAB profiles via postcard, phone, or video depending on category/region. Complete fully — unverified profiles rarely rank.

### Verification methods (what to expect)

| Method | How it works | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Postcard** | Card with code mailed to your address (5–14 days). Enter code in GBP. | Ensure address is deliverable; name on mailbox matches. Don't request multiple codes (resets timer). |
| **Phone / SMS** | Automated call/SMS to your GBP phone number with code. | Phone must be answered by you; don't use a call centre. |
| **Email** | Code sent to business email. | Check spam; use a domain email if possible. |
| **Video verification** | Live or recorded video showing: business location exterior, signage/entrance, equipment/tools, proof you operate there. | For home studios: show street → entrance → workshop bench + tools + pieces + ID. Film in one take; good lighting; no faces of family members. |
| **Instant verification** | If you already use Google Search Console on the same domain, sometimes instant. | Verify website in Search Console first if you have a site. |

### Verification playbook (do this in order)
1. [ ] Complete profile 100% (name, category, service area, phone, hours, description, photos) **before** requesting verification — incomplete profiles get rejected.
2. [ ] Request verification; choose the method offered (you may be offered only one).
3. [ ] If postcard: wait the full window; don't re-request. If it doesn't arrive in 14 days, contact Google Business support.
4. [ ] If video: prepare a 60–90 sec walkthrough script; rehearse; film in good light; upload via the GBP app.
5. [ ] After verification, **do not change name/address/phone** for 2–3 weeks — edits can re-trigger verification.
6. [ ] Add managers carefully (only trusted family/staff); owner account stays primary.

### If verification fails
- Re-read the rejection reason; usually "not enough evidence" (video) or "address mismatch" (postcard).
- For video: reshoot with clearer exterior → entrance → bench → tools → pieces sequence.
- For postcard: confirm address formatting matches postal records; try again after 14 days.
- Contact Google Business support via the help widget; have your business docs ready (CIPC registration, jeweller's permit).

## 7. Service Areas

As a SAB, you don't show an address — you define where you serve.

### Service-area setup
| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Type | City + surrounding suburbs (not a radius pin). Google prefers named places. |
| Primary city | Your city (e.g., Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban, Pretoria). |
| Suburbs/areas | List 10–20 key suburbs where your clients live (e.g., Constantia, Fourways, Umhlanga, Brooklyn). |
| Radius alternative | If your tool shows radius: 25 km (urban) or 50 km (metro/rural). Don't exceed 50 km — Google may ignore or penalise over-broad areas. |
| National shipping | Mention "nationwide shipping via courier" in description/services, but keep service area to your metro for ranking; don't list all 9 provinces. |

### Suggested city/suburb lists (examples)
| City | Suburbs to include |
|---|---|
| Cape Town | City Bowl, Sea Point, Green Point, Constantia, Tokai, Hout Bay, Gardens, Woodstock, Brackenfell, Durbanville, Somerset West, Stellenbosch (if served). |
| Johannesburg | Sandton, Rosebank, Fourways, Bryanston, Illovo, Norwood, Melville, Soweto (if served), Midrand, Randburg, Bedfordview. |
| Durban | Umhlanga, Durban North, Morningside, Westville, Hillcrest, Kloof, Ballito (if served). |
| Pretoria | Brooklyn, Hatfield, Menlyn, Waterkloof, Centurion, Lynnwood. |

### Rules
- **Don't** set service area to "South Africa" — too broad, won't rank locally.
- **Don't** claim suburbs you won't travel to or courier to — mismatched service area hurts trust.
- Update service area if you move studio or expand courier reach.
- Add "by appointment" and "courier nationwide" language so out-of-area searchers still convert.

## 8. Attributes

Attributes are yes/no flags that appear on your profile and in search filters. Tick only what's true.

### Recommended attributes for a home-based jeweler
| Attribute | Set to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Women-owned | Yes (if applicable) | Filter used by conscious buyers; trust signal. |
| Online appointments | Yes | Matches by-appointment studio model; enables booking intent. |
| Free consultations | Yes (if you offer a free first consult) | Reduces friction for bespoke commissions. |
| LGBTQ+ friendly | Yes (if applicable) | Inclusive trust signal. |
| Identifies as Black-owned / etc. | As applicable | Community trust + directory filters. |
| Wheelchair accessible entrance | Only if true | Don't tick if home studio has steps. |
| Appointment required | Yes | Sets correct expectation; avoids walk-ins. |
| Online estimates / quotes | Yes (if you quote via WhatsApp) | Matches your WhatsApp-first workflow. |
| Curbside pickup / Delivery | Delivery: Yes (courier) | Reflects nationwide shipping. |

### Attributes to avoid / not applicable
- **"In-store shopping"** / **"In-store pickup"** — don't tick if no retail shopfront (misleading; can trigger suspension).
- **"Walk-ins welcome"** — false for by-appointment studio.
- **"Restroom" / "Parking"** — not applicable to home studio; don't tick.

### Attributes hygiene
- Review attributes after any change in how you operate (e.g., if you start accepting walk-ins at a pop-up).
- Google periodically adds new attributes (e.g., "Recycled materials", "Sustainable") — re-check quarterly and tick if true (supports your ethical/recycled-gold narrative).

## 10. Weekly Posting Plan (GBP Posts)

Google Posts appear in search and on your profile. Cadence: **1–2 posts/week**. Each post: photo + 100–300 words + CTA (WhatsApp link).

### Weekly template (rotate)

| Day | Post type | Content | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | "On the bench" update | Photo of current commission (with permission) + 1–2 sentence story + craft detail. | "WhatsApp to commission yours" |
| Thu | Education / trust | Tip (e.g., "How to tell if your gold is 9ct vs 18ct", "When to resize vs remake"). | "Save this / DM questions" |
| (Optional) Sat | Offer / event | Seasonal promo (e.g., "Valentine's custom ring slots — 3 left"), market/pop-up dates, open-studio invite. | "Book via WhatsApp" |

### Post rules
- **Always include a photo** (no text-only posts).
- **CTA button:** "Call", "Learn more", or "Sign up" — link to WhatsApp click-to-chat (`wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20I%20found%20you%20on%20Google`).
- **No phone numbers in post text** (Google may reject); use the CTA link.
- **No pricing in posts that changes weekly** — keep posts evergreen or clearly dated.
- **Repost wins:** if a post got strong engagement, re-run a variation monthly.
- **Track:** note which post type drove WhatsApp enquiries; double down on winners.

### Monthly refresh (15 min)
- [ ] Add 8–12 new photos (2–3/week).
- [ ] Publish 4–8 Google Posts.
- [ ] Reply to all new reviews (§11).
- [ ] Update hours for holidays (e.g., closed over Dec/Jan).
- [ ] Check Q&A for new public questions; answer within 24 h.
- [ ] Review insights: views, searches, calls, direction requests, website clicks.

## 11. Review Strategy

Reviews are the #1 trust signal for a home-based jeweler (no shopfront = no foot traffic proof). Target: **10+ reviews in Month 1**, then steady velocity.

### Asking for reviews (WhatsApp script)
> "Hi [Name], so glad you love the [piece/repair]! If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean the world to a small home studio — it's how new clients find me. Here's the direct link: [short link]. No pressure at all, and thank you again. — [Your name]"

### Review link setup
- In GBP → "Get more reviews" → copy the short link (g.page/…).
- Shorten further with bit.ly or your own domain; put it in WhatsApp quick replies, email signature, Instagram bio, and on printed care cards.

### When to ask
| Trigger | Timing |
|---|---|
| After collection of a finished piece | Same day or next day (peak delight). |
| After a successful repair return | Within 48 h. |
| After a valuation certificate delivery | Within 1 week. |
| After a market/pop-up sale | Same day (while excitement is high). |
| After a referral converts | Within 1 week. |

### Rules
- **Never** buy or incentivise reviews (Google policy; can get profile suspended). A "thank you" gift after a review is fine; a discount *in exchange for* a review is not.
- **Ask every happy client** — review velocity matters more than a few perfect 5-stars.
- **Reply to every review** (positive and negative) within 48 h — responses are a ranking factor and show future clients you care.
- **Negative review playbook:** acknowledge, apologise for the experience (not necessarily fault), offer to fix offline via WhatsApp, then move on. Never argue publicly. Example: "I'm sorry this didn't meet your expectations, [Name]. Please WhatsApp me on +27… so we can make it right."

### Review response templates
| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| 5-star with detail | "Thank you, [Name]! So glad the [piece] turned out exactly as you imagined. It was a pleasure working on it — enjoy wearing it. — [Your name]" |
| 5-star short | "Thank you, [Name]! Appreciate you taking the time. — [Your name]" |
| 4-star | "Thanks, [Name]! Glad you're happy overall. If there's anything we can improve, please WhatsApp me. — [Your name]" |
| 3-star or below | "I'm sorry to hear this, [Name]. I'd love to make it right — please WhatsApp me on +27… so we can talk. — [Your name]" |

### Review velocity targets
| Period | Target |
|---|---|
| Month 1 | 10+ reviews, avg ≥ 4.7★ |
| Month 3 | 25+ reviews |
| Month 6 | 50+ reviews |
| Ongoing | 2–4 new reviews/month (steady velocity > bursts). |

## 12. Tracking & Optimization

### GBP Insights to watch (monthly)
| Metric | What it tells you | Action if low |
|---|---|---|
| Search views (direct vs discovery) | Whether people find you by name or by category search. | Low discovery → improve categories, description, photos, service area. |
| Map views | Local visibility. | Low → check service area, NAP, verification status. |
| Website clicks | Traffic to IG/site. | Low → improve cover photo, description CTA. |
| Calls / WhatsApp clicks | Lead volume. | Low → add "Call" CTA, post more, ask for reviews. |
| Direction requests | (Will be low for SAB — that's expected.) | N/A for home studio. |
| Photo views | Engagement. | Low → add fresh photos weekly. |

### Monthly optimization checklist
- [ ] Review insights; note top search queries.
- [ ] Add 8–12 new photos.
- [ ] Publish 4–8 Google Posts.
- [ ] Reply to all reviews and Q&A.
- [ ] Update services/prices if changed.
- [ ] Re-check categories and attributes for new options.
- [ ] Audit NAP across directories (fix any drift).
- [ ] Compare lead volume vs. prior month; adjust posting cadence.

## 13. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing home address as storefront | Privacy/safety risk; possible suspension. | Use SAB mode; hide address; "by appointment". |
| Keyword-stuffed business name ("Thabo Jeweler Cape Town") | Profile suspension. | Exact brand name only. |
| Unverified profile | Rarely ranks; wasted effort. | Complete verification first. |
| Claiming services not offered | Removal/suspension. | Only tick real services/attributes. |
| Inconsistent NAP across platforms | Ranking penalty. | Standardise and audit monthly. |
| Buying reviews / incentivising | Suspension; loss of trust. | Ask genuinely; reply to all. |
| Ignoring negative reviews | Signals poor service. | Reply within 48 h; fix offline. |
| Setting service area to "South Africa" | Won't rank locally. | City + suburbs, ≤50 km. |
| No photos / stale photos | Low engagement; lower ranking. | 10+ at launch; 2–3/week ongoing. |
| Ticking "walk-ins welcome" for home studio | Mismatched expectations; bad reviews. | "By appointment" only. |

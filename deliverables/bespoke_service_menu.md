## 1. About the Service

Bespoke and heirloom work is the highest-margin, lowest-material-risk line in the studio. The client either supplies their own gold and stones (heirloom) or commissions a new piece from scratch. Every job is **hand-fabricated at the bench** — no mass production, no casting-house shortcuts unless the design demands it (outsourced on request).

**Signature promise:** *"Heirloom redesign & bespoke rings — 30+ years at the bench."*
**Differentiators:** decades of bench skill · transparent per-gram pricing · remote-first service (WhatsApp video + courier) · heirloom upcycling as a signature line · lower overhead than studio houses.

## 5. Consultation Flow

**Free first consultation — 45–90 minutes.** By appointment only (in-person or WhatsApp video). The consultation IS the sale.

| Step | Activity | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discovery / Lead** — IG/FB/WhatsApp/referral; client shares inspiration photos + budget | Client + Jeweler | Enquiry |
| 2 | **Consultation** — story, budget, metal, stones, lifestyle, timeline; inspect heirloom pieces | Jeweler | Brief + budget range |
| 3 | **Stone Sourcing** (if applicable) — present certified diamond/gemstone options (natural / lab-grown / moissanite); client selects | Jeweler | Stone invoice/quotation |
| 4 | **Design** — hand sketch → CAD render (Rhino/MATRIX) → 3D render images; **2 revisions included** | Jeweler/CAD | Render set |
| 5 | **Approval + Deposit** — client approves render; signs commission agreement; pays 50% (or full) deposit | Both | Signed contract + deposit receipt |
| 6 | **Fabrication** — wax model → cast (outsourced if needed) → stone setting → finishing/polishing; heirloom: melt, refine, re-alloy | Bench jeweler | Finished piece |
| 7 | **Quality Check** — setting security, polish, sizing, engraving | Jeweler | QC sign-off |
| 8 | **Delivery / Presentation** — in-person handover or courier (tracked, insured); reveal moment; resizing window | Both | Delivered piece |
| 9 | **Aftercare** — free first resize (often), cleaning, polishing, warranty on workmanship | Jeweler | Repeat client |

**Key rules:**
- **Consultation is the sale.** Free first consult; design fee (if charged, R500–R2,500) is **credited to the final piece**.
- **CAD render approval is the risk gate** — no fabrication before written approval.
- **Heirloom intake** requires an identification/appraisal step **before** quoting (ZHIVA principle: "A good redesign starts with a conversation, not a sketch").
- **Remote clients** are served via WhatsApp video calls + photo/video of renders + courier delivery — standard practice, expands market beyond CT/JHB.

**Design communication tools:** hand sketches (pencil on vellum, photographed for WhatsApp) · CAD (Rhino + RhinoArtisan, or outsourced freelance CAD) · 3D-printed wax model for physical approval · WhatsApp (dominant) · email (formal quotes/contracts) · video calls (screen-share renders).

## 7. Pricing Formula (SA-Adapted)

```
Total Price = Metal cost (grams × rate by carat)
            + Stone cost (carat × price/ct, incl. cert premium)
            + Making charge (labor: hourly or % of metal)
            + Design fee (consultation/CAD, often credited)
            + Setting/stone-handling fee
            + Overhead & profit margin
            + VAT (15%) if registered
            − Scrap credit (client's old gold, at trade scrap value)
```

## 8. Terms & Conditions (Outline)

*Full T&Cs to be drafted with a SA attorney; this is the working outline.*

1. **Definitions** — "Jeweler", "Client", "Commission", "Piece", "Deposit".
2. **Scope of work** — description of the piece, metal, stones, design render reference (attached).
3. **Quotation & price** — spot-based; valid 7 days; price-escalation clause for jobs > 4 weeks; VAT if registered.
4. **Deposit** — 50% non-refundable before production; balance before delivery; no refunds on confirmed custom orders (Consumer Protection Act disclosure).
5. **Design approval** — written approval (email/WhatsApp) of CAD render required before fabrication; 2 revisions included; further revisions billable.
6. **Changes / scope creep** — any change after approval requires a written re-quote and may delay delivery.
7. **Timeline** — target delivery date; not guaranteed; jeweler communicates proactively if slipping; force majeure (metal/stone supply delays).
8. **Heirloom intake** — client warrants ownership of supplied pieces; jeweler documents condition with photos at intake; metal loss in melting (5–10%) disclosed; stone damage risk disclosed in writing; replacement sourcing offered if damage occurs.
9. **Stone sourcing** — only certified suppliers; GIA/HRD/IGI certs for diamonds > 0.30 ct; lab-grown vs natural disclosed in writing; never substituted; stones held in client's name until paid.
10. **Ownership & IP** — design/CAD files remain jeweler's IP unless paid for; finished piece becomes client's property on full payment.
11. **Delivery** — in-person handover or courier (insured, tracked, signature on delivery); piece photographed before shipping; risk passes on delivery.
12. **Resizing / aftercare** — one free resize within 30 days; cleaning/polishing available; warranty on workmanship (e.g., 6 months on setting security).
13. **Insurance** — client advised to insure high-value pieces; jeweler carries business policy for premises/transit.
14. **Dispute resolution** — good-faith negotiation first; then mediation; jurisdiction: courts of [city], South Africa.
15. **POPIA** — client data (photos, addresses) protected; used only for the commission and marketing with written consent.
16. **Compliance** — CIPC registration if scaling; VAT if turnover > R1M; hallmarking/assay per Precious Metals Act; second-hand goods records for heirloom intake.

# Marketing Channels Playbook — South African Home-Based Jeweler (49 Years Experience)

**Prepared:** October 2026 | **Operator profile:** Home-based jeweler, ~49 years experience, selling bespoke/handcrafted jewelry in South Africa.
**Goal:** Turn 49 years of craft credibility into a steady inbound pipeline across search, social, messaging, partnerships and offline events.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — Setup & Optimization for SA

A Google Business Profile is the single highest-ROI free channel for a home-based jeweler because SA customers search "jeweler near me", "custom ring Cape Town/Johannesburg/Durban" and "jewelry repair [city]". Even without a shopfront, Google lets you list as a **Service-Area Business (SAB)**.

### Setup steps
1. Go to **business.google.com** → "Manage now" → create profile with your exact business name (match your WhatsApp/Instagram name).
2. Choose primary category **"Jeweler"**; add secondary categories such as "Jewelry repair service", "Custom jewelry designer", "Watch repair service" if applicable.
3. Because you work from home, select **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** and define a **service area** (city + surrounding suburbs / 25–50 km radius). Do **not** publish your home address publicly (Google's SAB guidelines).
4. Verification: Google will verify via postcard, phone, or video depending on category/region. Complete it fully — unverified profiles rarely rank.
5. Add phone (WhatsApp-capable), website/Instagram link, business hours, and a short description loaded with natural keywords ("custom engagement rings, heirloom restoration, gold/silver repairs, 49 years experience").

### Optimization checklist (weekly)
- **Photos (10+):** workshop bench, tools, before/after repairs, finished pieces on hand, packaging. Google favours fresh photos; add 2–3 per week.
- **Posts (1–2/week):** "What's on the bench" updates, new commissions, Google Posts with a CTA (WhatsApp link).
- **Reviews:** ask every happy client via WhatsApp with a direct review link. Reply to **every** review (positive and negative) — review velocity + responses are ranking factors.
- **Q&A:** seed your own Q&A ("Do you do custom engagement rings?", "Can I courier my jewelry?").
- **Attributes:** "Women-owned", "Online appointments", "Free consultations" where true.
- **NAP consistency:** Name/Address/Phone identical across Instagram bio, Facebook, website, directories (Yelp SA, Hotfrog, Brabys, SA Weddings supplier listings).

## 2. Instagram & Facebook Strategy

Instagram is the #1 visual sales channel for jewelry; Facebook carries older/higher-income SA buyers and community groups.

### Profile optimization
- Handle: `@[name]jewellery` or `@[name]jeweller` (consistent across platforms).
- Bio: "49 yrs making bespoke & heirloom jewelry in [City], SA · Custom rings · Repairs · DM/WhatsApp to commission".
- Link-in-bio: WhatsApp click-to-chat (`wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX`) + catalog link.
- Highlights: *Custom Work, Repairs, Heirloom Stories, Reviews, Process, Prices/FAQ*.

### Content mix (4 pillars)
| Pillar | % | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Process / behind-the-file | 35% | Filing, soldering, stone setting, polishing, "day in the workshop" |
| Product showcase | 25% | Macro shots, on-hand, 360°, before/after |
| Story / heirloom | 25% | "This ring belonged to..." transformations, client testimonials |
| Education / trust | 15% | "How to care for gold", "What 9ct vs 18ct means", gemstone guides |

### Reels (the reach engine)
- Post **4–5 Reels/week**, 7–15 sec, vertical 9:16, native captions.
- Hooks in first 2 seconds: "Watch a 49-year-old ring come back to life", "POV: you're a jeweler at 6am".
- Use trending SA audio sparingly; original workshop sounds (hammer, torch) perform well for craft content.
- Always CTA: "WhatsApp me to commission yours" + link sticker.

### Hashtag sets (rotate, 8–12 per post)
- Location: `#CapeTownJeweller #JohannesburgJeweller #DurbanJeweller #PretoriaJeweller #SouthAfricanJeweller #SAJeweller`
- Craft: `#HandmadeJewelry #HandcraftedJewelry #BespokeJewelry #CustomJewelry #HeirloomJewelry #JewelryMaker #BehindTheBench`
- Niche: `#EngagementRingSA #WeddingRingSA #Goldsmith #Silversmith #JewelryRepair #JewelryRestoration`
- Broad: `#JewelryLovers #JewelryOfTheDay #JewelryDesign`

### Facebook
- Mirror Reels as FB Reels (free cross-post reach).
- Post to local community groups (see §6) 2–3x/week with value, not spam.
- Use FB Events for open-studio/pop-up days.

## 3. TikTok for Jewelry (Process Content)

TikTok's algorithm rewards raw, satisfying process video — perfect for a 49-year veteran. Younger SA buyers (engagement rings, gifts) discover brands here.

### Content ideas that work
- "Satisfying" macros: filing, polishing, stone setting, engraving, wax carving.
- "Storytime" voiceovers over a repair: "A client brought me her grandmother's ring…"
- "Day in the life of a jeweler" with POV shots.
- Duets/stitches of trending jewelry questions.
- Before/after reveals with a beat drop.

### Strategy
- Post **1/day** for the first 30 days to train the algorithm; then 3–4/week.
- Hook in 1–2 sec; keep 7–20 sec; loop-friendly endings.
- Captions with searchable phrases: "custom engagement ring South Africa", "jewelry repair Cape Town".
- Bio link → WhatsApp. Use TikTok's "Catalog"/shopping if eligible.
- Repurpose every TikTok as an IG Reel and YT Short (one shoot, three platforms).

## 4. WhatsApp Business (Catalog, Broadcasts, Status)

WhatsApp is the default commerce channel in SA. Treat it as your shopfront + CRM.

### Setup
- Download **WhatsApp Business** (free) on a dedicated number (or dual-SIM).
- Complete business profile: name, category "Jewelry", description, hours, address (or "by appointment"), website/IG link, email.
- Enable **Quick Replies** ("Thanks for enquiring — here's my catalog"), **Greeting Message**, **Away Message** (after hours).
- **Labels:** New Lead, Quoting, In Progress, Ready for Collection, Paid, Repeat Client, VIP.

### Catalog (up to 500 items)
- Photograph each piece on white/neutral background + on-hand; add price (or "POA for custom"), description, link.
- Group by collection: Engagement, Everyday, Heirloom Restorations, Repairs, Custom Commissions.
- Share catalog link in bio, GBP, email signature.

### Broadcasts & Status
- **Status:** post 3–5x/week — workshop moments, new pieces, "last slot this month" urgency. Status views are high-intent.
- **Broadcast lists** (not groups): segment by interest (Brides, Repair clients, Collectors, Wholesale). Send 1–2x/month max — value, not spam. Respect POPIA: only message opted-in contacts; include opt-out.
- **Catalogue link sharing:** send a direct product link in chat so clients browse without leaving WhatsApp.

## 5. Referral Networks (Salons, Boutiques, Interior Designers, Wedding Planners)

B2B referrals convert at the highest rate and cost almost nothing. Build a "preferred partner" roster.

### Target partners
- **Wedding planners & coordinators** (highest value): bridal jewelry, engagement rings, wedding bands, mother-of-bride pieces. List on Pink Book (pink-book.co.za) and SA Weddings (saweddings.co.za) directories.
- **Bridal boutiques / dress shops:** reciprocal referral cards; leave a small display of earrings/pendants.
- **Salons & spas:** gift cards for anniversaries/birthdays; display case on reception counter.
- **Interior designers & home stagers:** jewelry as decor props; co-host trunk shows.
- **Photographers & videographers:** styled shoots credit; real-wedding features tag you.
- **Florists & cake designers:** bundled "wedding vendor" packages.
- **Estate agents / property stagers:** gifting for new homeowners (locket/pendant).

### Activation playbook
1. Identify 20 target partners in your city via Instagram + Google.
2. Send a personal WhatsApp/DM: "I'm a 49-year jeweler; I'd love to be your trusted jewelry partner. Can I drop off a sample + referral cards?"
3. Offer **10–15% referral commission** or reciprocal referrals; provide a unique discount code per partner to track attribution.
4. Deliver a "partner kit": 20 referral cards, a display tray, care cards, WhatsApp link QR.
5. Follow up monthly; invite partners to open-studio events.

## 7. Paid Ads (Facebook/Instagram/Google) — Budgets in ZAR

Start small, measure, scale winners. Recommended Phase-1 budget: **R3,000–R6,000/month** total.

### Meta (FB/IG) Ads
- **Budget:** R50–R150/day per ad set (≈R1,500–R4,500/month). Start at R50/day for 7 days to gather data.
- **Objective:** Leads (WhatsApp click-to-chat) or Sales (if catalog linked). Avoid "Boost post" for cold traffic; use Ads Manager.
- **Audience targeting:**
  - Location: your city + 25 km.
  - Age: 22–55; gender split by product (women 25–45 for gifting; men 25–40 for engagement rings).
  - Interests: "engagement ring", "wedding", "jewelry", "luxury goods", "bridal", "anniversary".
  - Life events: "recently engaged", "upcoming anniversary", "newlywed".
  - Retargeting: website/IG engagers (custom audience) — highest ROI.
- **Creative:** Reels-style video (process + finished piece), carousel of 3–5 pieces, UGC-style testimonials. Hook in 2 sec.
- **Benchmarks:** jewelry FB CPC ≈ $1.07 (~R19–R20), CTR ≈ 1.14%. Expect R20–R40 CPC in SA depending on competition. Aim for R80–R200 cost per WhatsApp lead.
- **Budget tiers (USD→ZAR guide):** affordable pieces → ~$10–30/day; mid-range → ~$30–70/day; luxury → $100–300/day. For a home jeweler, stay at the affordable/mid tier.

### Google Ads (Search)
- **Budget:** R2,000–R5,000/month to start (RCCI guidance for SA small business).
- **Campaign type:** Search only (skip Display initially). Keywords: "custom engagement ring [city]", "jewelry repair [city]", "bespoke jewelry [city]", "jeweler near me".
- **Match types:** phrase/exact for high-intent; broad only with strong negatives.
- **Negatives:** "cheap", "wholesale", "free", "costume", competitor brand names (unless conquest intended).
- **Benchmarks SA:** consumer services CPC ≈ R12.42 (procompare); jewelry/apparel CPC ≈ $4.44 (~R80) globally (Quimby 2026). SA jewelry CPCs typically R15–R60 for local terms. Target R150–R400 cost per lead.
- **Extensions:** call, location (service area), sitelinks (Catalog, Repairs, Custom, Reviews), callout ("49 yrs experience").

### Measurement
- Track: cost per lead (WhatsApp), cost per sale, ROAS. Kill ad sets with no leads after R300 spend; scale winners by 20%/week.

## 8. Content Ideas That Resonate

Stories sell jewelry. Your 49 years is the moat — make it the content.

### High-performing concepts
- **Heirloom stories:** "This ring was my client's grandmother's. Here's how we restored it." (emotional, shareable)
- **Behind-the-file / bench POV:** raw workshop footage, no music, just sounds.
- **Transformation reveals:** before → after with a beat drop.
- **"49 years of…" series:** one lesson per post ("Why I never work on Friday afternoons", "The tool I've used for 30 years").
- **Client spotlights:** engagement stories, anniversary gifts, repair miracles (with permission).
- **Education:** "9ct vs 18ct vs 22ct — what's the difference?", "How to tell if your gold is real", "When to resize vs remake".
- **Myth-busting:** "No, you shouldn't clean diamonds with toothpaste."
- **Price transparency:** "What R3,500 gets you in a custom ring" (builds trust).
- **Process ASMR:** close-up polishing, engraving, stone setting.
- **"Ask the jeweler" Q&A:** answer DMs on camera.

## 9. Influencer / Micro-Influencer (SA Jewelry Space)

Nano (1K–10K) and micro (10K–50K) creators outperform big accounts on engagement and cost. Prioritize **gifting + commission** over cash fees.

### SA rate card (ZAR) — from usesnippet.app
| Tier | Followers | Sponsored post | Video/Reel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1K–10K | R500–R2,500 | R1,000–R5,000 |
| Micro | 10K–50K | R2,500–R12,000 | R5,000–R25,000 |
| Mid | 50K–500K | R12,000–R50,000 | R25,000–R100,000 |

For a home jeweler, target **nano/micro lifestyle, bridal, fashion and "mom" creators** in your city (engagement 3–8%). Many accept gifted pieces + affiliate commission (10–15%).

### Outreach playbook
1. Build a list of 30 creators (engagement rate >3%, audience matches your city).
2. DM: compliment specific post → offer gifted piece → propose a Reel + Story + link sticker → ask for usage rights for ads.
3. Send a "creator kit": piece, care card, brand story, hashtag list, WhatsApp link.
4. Repurpose their content as ads (with permission) — best-performing creative usually comes from creators.
5. Track with unique discount codes; pay per performance where possible.

## 10. Offline: Markets, Pop-Ups, Bridal Fairs

Offline events build trust fastest for a craft business and create content. Budget R500–R3,000 per event for a stall.

### Craft markets (SA)
- **Kamers/Makers** (multiple cities) — curated, higher footfall, higher fees.
- **The Linden Market** (Johannesburg) — strong artisan crowd.
- **Bay Harbour Market** (Cape Town, Hout Bay) — weekend market, good for jewelry.
- **Neighbourgoods Market** (Johannesburg/Cape Town) — premium, Saturday crowds.
- **I Heart Market** (Durban) — strong craft/food mix.
- **Bryanston Organic Market** (Johannesburg) — established, loyal.
- **Oranjezicht City Farm Market** (Cape Town) — weekend, affluent crowd.
- Application fees typically R0–R50; booth fees R200–R1,500+ depending on juried status.

### Bridal fairs / wedding expos
- **SA Bridal Fairs** (sabridalfairs.com) — central listing of SA expos.
- **Oakfield Farm Bridal Expo** (June) — 100+ exhibitors, Gauteng.
- **Cape Town Winter Wedding Expo** (Kirstenbosch) — June.
- **Say "I Do" Bridal Expo** (Brooklyn Mall, Pretoria) — Oct.
- **The Wedding Expo** (Johannesburg) — June.
- **Fab Bride Expo** (Cape Town) — August.
- **SA Wedding Fair** (Durban) — October.
- Booth fees vary (typically R2,000–R8,000+); prioritize expos with bride attendance in your city.

### Pop-ups & open studio
- Host a **quarterly open-studio day** ("Meet the maker", tea, live demos) — invite partners and past clients.
- Co-host trunk shows with bridal boutiques/salons (they provide venue + audience).
- Corporate gifting pitches (December): bulk pendants/engraved pieces for companies.

## 11. 30-Day Launch Plan (Daily Tasks)

Assume starting from zero. Each day ≈ 60–90 min. Adjust to your capacity.

### Week 1 — Foundations
- **Day 1:** Define brand name, handle, one-line positioning ("49-year SA jeweler — bespoke, heirloom, repair"). Set up WhatsApp Business number + profile.
- **Day 2:** Create Instagram + Facebook pages; write bios; link WhatsApp click-to-chat; set up highlights covers.
- **Day 3:** Create Google Business Profile (SAB, service area). Start verification.
- **Day 4:** Build WhatsApp catalog (first 20 pieces: photos, prices, descriptions).
- **Day 5:** Shoot 15 photos + 5 short videos (process + finished pieces) on phone.
- **Day 6:** Write 30 content captions in a bank; plan hashtag sets.
- **Day 7:** Review GBP verification; ask 5 past clients for Google reviews.

### Week 2 — Content Engine
- **Day 8:** Post first Reel (process) + FB cross-post. Post WhatsApp Status.
- **Day 9:** Post second Reel (heirloom story) + 3 photos. Join 5 FB community groups.
- **Day 10:** Post third Reel (education). Send partner outreach to 5 wedding planners.
- **Day 11:** Post TikTok (first). Reply to all comments/DMs within 1 hour.
- **Day 12:** Post Reel (before/after repair). Send partner kit to 2 salons.
- **Day 13:** Post FB group value post (jewelry care tip). Post WhatsApp Status x2.
- **Day 14:** Weekly review: which post got most saves/shares? Note top format. Plan next week.

### Week 3 — Partnerships & Paid
- **Day 15:** Apply to 2 craft markets for next month.
- **Day 16:** Apply to 1 bridal fair; list on Pink Book / SA Weddings directories.
- **Day 17:** Set up Meta Ads: 1 ad set, R50/day, WhatsApp objective, 3 creatives.
- **Day 18:** Set up Google Ads Search: R100/day, 5 keywords, 3 ad variations.
- **Day 19:** Post Reel (49-years lesson). Outreach to 5 more creators (gifting).
- **Day 20:** Host a mini open-studio (invite 5 past clients + 2 partners).
- **Day 21:** Weekly review: ad spend, leads, cost per lead. Pause losers.

### Week 4 — Scale & Systemize
- **Day 22:** Post best-performing Reel format again (variation). Add 10 more catalog items.
- **Day 23:** Send first WhatsApp broadcast to segmented list (value + new piece).
- **Day 24:** Post TikTok + IG Reel. Pitch 3 corporate gifting prospects (Dec season).
- **Day 25:** Collect 3 video testimonials from happy clients.
- **Day 26:** Refresh GBP: new photos, a Google Post, reply to all reviews.
- **Day 27:** Co-host a trunk show with a bridal boutique.
- **Day 28:** Review month: revenue, leads, top channel, top content, top partner.
- **Day 29:** Decide scale: increase ad budget 20% on winner; book next market.
- **Day 30:** Document SOPs (content batch day, weekly review, partner follow-up). Set 90-day goals.

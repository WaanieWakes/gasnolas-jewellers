# Website & Ecommerce Strategy — South African Home-Based Jeweler (49 Years)

**Prepared:** October 2026 | **Focus:** Home-based SA jeweler, handcrafted jewelry, 49 years experience, selling primarily to South African buyers with potential international reach.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

For a home-based South African jeweler with 49 years of craft and a modest catalogue (rings, bracelets, earrings, necklaces), the recommended path is a **mobile-first, 6-page site on Shopify (Basic) or Shopstar** with PayFast as the primary gateway, Bob Go for shipping, and WhatsApp as the conversion/comms layer. WooCommerce is the lower-cost alternative if you already run WordPress and can handle maintenance. Avoid Squarespace (weak SA gateway support) and pure Carrd (no real cart). Launch with local trust signals (49 Years badge, "Handcrafted in South Africa", Google reviews) and local SEO targeting "[city] jeweller" queries.

**Key SA realities:**
- **Shopify Payments is NOT available in SA.** South African Shopify stores must use a third-party gateway AND pay Shopify's platform fee (2% Basic → 1% → 0.6% on higher plans). Stacking PayFast (3.2%+R2) + Shopify 2% = ~5%+ per sale on Basic.
- **Stripe points SA businesses to Paystack** (Stripe subsidiary, acquired 2020). Stripe itself is "extended network" only.
- **Payment methods SA buyers expect:** cards (3DS), Instant EFT (Ozow/Capitec Pay/Absa Pay), Apple/Google/Samsung Pay, SnapScan/Zapper, BNPL (Mobicred/MoreTyme) for items >R1,000, and increasingly cash-at-tills (Pay@).
- **PayFast payout fee** of R8.70 per withdrawal — withdraw monthly to minimize. Settlement 2–3 business days.
- **Yoco** has no payout fee, settles T+2, simpler onboarding, good if you already use a Yoco card machine.

**Recommendation for a jeweler:** PayFast (widest SA method coverage + buyer trust + subscriptions) as primary; add **Ozow** for cheaper EFT on high-value pieces; consider **Yoco** if you also sell in person at markets. If selling internationally, add Paystack or Stripe.

## 4. Shipping & Courier Integration (SA)

**Aggregator of choice: Bob Go** (formerly uAfrica, part of Bob Group) — connects to The Courier Guy, Pargo, RAM, SkyNet, Internet Express, Fastway, Bob Box lockers. Integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, Ecwid, Magento. Rates at checkout, auto-labels, tracking emails.

**Major SA couriers:**
- **The Courier Guy** — overnight to major centres, economy 2–4 days, same-day in some areas; WooCommerce + Shopify plugins.
- **Aramex SA** — international + local; WooCommerce extension.
- **Fastway** — franchise depots, national satchels; Shopify/WooCommerce/Ecwid integrations.
- **Pargo** — 4,000+ pickup points (click-and-collect), cheaper; Shopify + WooCommerce plugins.
- **PUDO** — 24/7 smart lockers (The Courier Guy); collect with a code.
- **PostNet** — 500+ stores, counter-to-counter.
- **DHL Express / FedEx** — international (for overseas orders).
- **Uber Connect / Bolt Send** — same-day local, real-time pricing.

**Best practice:** Offer door-to-door (courier) + pickup-point/locker (cheaper) options at checkout. Use **Bob Go** to compare rates automatically. For a home jeweler, start with The Courier Guy + Pargo pickup option; add Bob Go once volume exceeds ~5 parcels/day.

**Source:** Bob Go integrations; Courier Guy; WebFootprint; AfriBiz Tech; DeliverAI 2026 SA courier API reference.

## 5. Trust Signals for SA Buyers

South African buyers are cautious (scams, unsecured gateways, poor delivery). For a 49-year home jeweler, trust is your biggest conversion lever.

**On-site trust signals:**
- **"49 Years of Craftsmanship" badge** — prominent on hero, about page, and product pages. Years-in-business is a top jewelry trust signal.
- **"Handcrafted in South Africa"** label + SA flag icon.
- **Named maker / face** — photo of you at the bench; personal story.
- **Google Reviews** — aim for 30+ reviews, 4.8+ stars; respond to every review within 48h.
- **Secure checkout badges** — SSL padlock + "Secure Checkout" label directly above payment form (not footer), plus PayFast/Visa/Mastercard/Ozow logos visible before checkout.
- **Clear returns policy** on product pages ("14-day return, original condition"), not just footer.
- **Delivery promise on product page** — "Ships within 2 business days; Joburg 3–5 days."
- **Social proof near CTA** — star rating + review count next to "Add to Cart."
- **Certifications** — if applicable: SA Jewellery Council membership, hallmark/quality marks, gemstone certificates.
- **WhatsApp number** displayed prominently (SA buyers trust a real person on WhatsApp).
- **Physical address / suburb** (even if home-based, list a suburb + city) + Google Maps embed.

**Source:** GrowthPulseMedia (trust signals for SA buyers); Flento (jewelry local SEO); EC Business Solutions (mobile-first local SEO).

## 6. Mobile-First (60%+ SA Searches on Phones)

- **60% of local searches happen on phones** (FreshStartDigital); **~70% of SA online shopping** is on smartphones (Wildfire SEO / Peach Payments).
- Google uses **mobile-first indexing** — your mobile site is what ranks.
- Targets: page load **<2.5s** on 3G/4G; responsive design (no pinching); tap-to-call + tap-for-directions; compressed images; clear thumb-friendly CTAs; one-page checkout.
- **Specific SA anxieties on mobile:** payment fraud awareness high, first-time mobile buyers, small screens hide credibility signals → place trust signals at decision points, not in footer.
- Test on real mid-range Android devices (most common in SA) and slow networks.

## 7. Local SEO for Jewelry ("Jeweller Near Me", City Keywords)

- **Google Business Profile (GBP)** is the #1 lever — complete it: 30+ photos, products/services enabled, hours, categories ("Jeweler", "Jewelry store", "Jewelry repair"), Q&A seeded.
- **Target keywords:** "jeweller [city]", "jewelry store near me [suburb]", "custom engagement rings [city]", "handmade jewelry [city]", "jewelry repair [city]".
- **NAP consistency** across Google, Yelp SA, Yellow Pages SA, Hotfrog, Cylex, SA Jewellery Council directory, local Chamber of Commerce.
- **Reviews:** post-purchase + post-repair + custom-piece completion (emotional high point) + anniversary reminders; QR code on packaging.
- **Schema markup:** LocalBusiness + Product schema (price, availability, reviews).
- **Content:** city/neighbourhood names in titles, H1s, meta; blog posts like "How to choose an engagement ring in [City]"; multilingual snippets (Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa) where relevant.
- **Geo-tagged images**, embedded Google Map on contact page, local backlinks (lifestyle publications, local blogs).
- **Audit existing citations** for NAP inconsistencies before building new ones (BrightLocal/Whitespark).

## 8. WhatsApp Commerce

WhatsApp is the default comms channel in SA (69% of SA retailers use it for support; 36% for sales).

**Approach:**
- **WhatsApp Business** (free) with catalogue, quick replies, labels, away messages — minimum viable.
- **WhatsApp Business API** via providers (OrderZA, Sokoleho, ChatWorks, SOLDT, LinkDeck) for automated catalogues, cart, payment links, order tracking — from R0–R399/month.
- **Meta Catalogue sync** from Shopify/WooCommerce so stock & prices stay accurate (hourly/near-real-time refresh).
- **Payment flow:** catalogue browse + cart in WhatsApp → PayFast/Yoco/Ozow payment link → order writes back to store. (Only ~17% of SA WhatsApp-for-sales retailers had tried payment links — big opportunity.)
- **Use cases for a jeweler:** daily "new piece" status updates, custom-order design approvals with photos, gold/silver rate broadcasts, appointment booking for consultations, repair status updates, review requests.

**Source:** WebFootprint (WhatsApp commerce catalogue); OrderZA; Sokoleho; ChatWorks; SOLDT; LinkDeck; WatEase (jewelry).

## 10. Recommended 6-Page Site Structure

1. **Home** — hero with "49 Years of Handcrafted Jewelry in [City], South Africa"; trust strip (49 Years badge, Handcrafted in SA, Secure Checkout, Free Delivery over R X); featured pieces; WhatsApp CTA; reviews carousel.
2. **Shop** — categories (Rings, Bracelets, Earrings, Necklaces, Custom/Bespoke, Repairs); filters; product cards with price, rating, "Ships in 2 days".
3. **Product page** — photos (incl. on-model/hand shots), materials, sizing guide, delivery promise, returns policy, reviews, "Ask on WhatsApp", Add to Cart.
4. **About / Our Story** — your 49-year journey, bench photo, "Handcrafted in South Africa", certifications, press/awards.
5. **Custom & Repairs** — bespoke commission process, consultation booking, repair services, turnaround times, gallery of past commissions.
6. **Contact** — WhatsApp button (primary), phone, email, suburb/city + Google Map, hours, FAQ (shipping, returns, sizing, care).

Plus: Cart/Checkout, Order Tracking, Privacy/POPIA, Terms, Shipping Policy, Returns Policy (footer links).

- [ ] (Optional) Open Yoco/Ozow for additional methods.

- [ ] Install SSL; verify mobile load speed <2.5s.

- [ ] Write NAP-consistent listings on Yelp SA, Yellow Pages, Hotfrog, Cylex, SA Jewellery Council.
- [ ] Install LocalBusiness + Product schema.

**Content (Weeks 2–3)**
- [ ] Photograph every piece (white background + lifestyle/on-hand); compress images.
- [ ] Write product descriptions with materials, dimensions, care, delivery promise.
- [ ] Create "49 Years" badge asset; "Handcrafted in South Africa" label.
- [ ] Draft returns/shipping/privacy (POPIA) policies.
- [ ] Seed FAQ page.

**Trust & Conversion (Week 3)**
- [ ] Add WhatsApp Business number + quick replies + catalogue.
- [ ] Place trust signals at decision points (checkout, product page).
- [ ] Set up review request flow (post-purchase email/WhatsApp with Google review link + QR on packaging).
- [ ] Install Meta Pixel + Google Analytics; set up conversion tracking.

**Launch (Week 4)**
- [ ] Place 3–5 test orders end-to-end (card, EFT, mobile, desktop).
- [ ] Test shipping label generation + tracking email.
- [ ] Soft launch to existing customers/WhatsApp contacts for first reviews.
- [ ] Announce on Instagram/Facebook/TikTok with link + QR code.
- [ ] Monitor Google Search Console for indexing; submit sitemap.

**Post-launch (ongoing)**
- [ ] Respond to every review within 48h.
- [ ] Publish 1–2 blog posts/month targeting city keywords.
- [ ] Add new pieces + update Meta catalogue weekly.
- [ ] Monthly: withdraw PayFast balance (minimize R8.70 fees); review conversion + speed.
- [ ] Quarterly: audit citations (NAP), refresh photos, review courier rates.

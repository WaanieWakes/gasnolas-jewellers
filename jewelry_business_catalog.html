4. WEBSITE IDEAS FOR DAD'S BUSINESS

4.1 Site Structure (6-page minimum)

  1. Home: "Four decades of handcrafted gold & silver — now from my home studio." Hero image of Dad at work, tagline.
  2. Gallery / Portfolio: Categorized grid: Name necklaces • Bespoke rings • Silver collections • Ethical pieces. High-res photos, each with short story caption ("Hand-filed 1977, set 2026").
  3. About / Story: "49 years in the making." Video or text about his journey, the tools he still uses, the transition to home studio.
  4. Shop / Collections: Ready-to-order pieces with clear pricing (gold/gram + making charge). Include "starting at" prices to qualify leads.
  5. Consult / Custom: Simple form: "Tell us your vision — necklaces, rings, rewriting a family heirloom." Capture name, contact, budget range, timeline.
  6. Contact: Address (home studio — be discrete but build trust), phone, email, WhatsApp (very SA-popular). Map.

4.2 Design & UX Priorities

  • Mobile-first: 60%+ of SA jewelry searches are on phones. Large pendant/necklace photos zoom well.
  • Trust signals: "49 years in business" badge, "Handcrafted in South Africa" logo, any press or gallery features.
  • Clear CTA: "Start your custom piece — WhatsApp consult" or "Book a video call."
  • Dark mode / light mode: Jewelry looks different under different lighting; offer both.
  • Image optimization: Photos must show true gold color. Use color-corrected studio lighting, not mixed sunlight/bulb.

4.3 Tech Stack (low-cost, no-code options)

  • Shopify + Jewelry theme: R1,000-R2,000/mo + theme. Handles payments, inventory, WhatsApp integration.
  • WordPress + WooCommerce + Elementor: R500-R1,000 setup, R200-R500/mo hosting. Full control.
  • Carrd + PayPal/Stripe: R150 one-time for simple brochure site + order forms. Good for starting.
  • Google Site + Calendly: Free, very limited. Good for absolute start-with-nothing.

5. MARKETING & GETTING CLIENTELE (Home-Based)

5.1 Low-Cost / Zero-Ad Spend Strategies

  • Google Business Profile: Set up as "Jeweller — handcrafted gold & silver." Appears in "near me" searches. Post weekly photos of new pieces.
  • Instagram & Facebook: Post process videos (short: "Filing a gold band from 1977"). Tag local businesses, use local hashtags: #CapeTownJeweller #JoburgGold #HandcraftedSA.
  • Referral network: Hair salons, fashion boutiques, interior designers — people who meet clients who might want jewelry. Offer 10% referral fee on first order.
  • Facebook Groups: Join local community groups (e.g., "Sandton Moms," "Claremont Home & Garden"). Share gently: "Custom heirloom pieces available from home studio — 49 years experience."
  • Word of mouth (structured): Give Dad 10 business cards. Ask every single person he meets: "Do you know someone looking for a one-of-a-kind gold piece?"

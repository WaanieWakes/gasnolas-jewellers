## 3. Customer Journey for Custom Pieces

Standard SA bespoke journey (synthesized from Olga, Prins & Prins, Dear Rae, Arias, Cassims, Prata, Black Betty):

| Stage | Activities | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery/Lead | IG/FB/WhatsApp/ referral; client shares inspiration photos, budget | Client + Jeweler | Enquiry |
| 2. Consultation (free, 45–90 min) | In-person or video call; discuss story, budget, metal, stones, lifestyle, timeline; inspect heirloom pieces | Jeweler | Brief + budget range |
| 3. Stone sourcing | Present certified diamond/gemstone options (natural/lab-grown/moissanite); client selects | Jeweler | Stone invoice/quotation |
| 4. Design | Hand sketches → CAD render (Rhino/MATRIX/3Design) → 3D render images; revisions (typically 2 included) | Jeweler/CAD | Render set |
| 5. Approval + deposit | Client approves render; signs commission agreement; pays 50% (or full) deposit | Both | Signed contract + deposit receipt |
| 6. Fabrication | Wax model/CAM → casting → stone setting → finishing/polishing; heirloom upcycling: melt, refine, re-alloy | Bench jeweler | Finished piece |
| 7. Quality check | Inspect setting security, polish, sizing, engraving | Jeweler | QC sign-off |
| 8. Delivery/presentation | In-person handover or courier (tracked, insured); reveal moment; resizing window | Both | Delivered piece |
| 9. Aftercare | Free first resize (often), cleaning, polishing, warranty on setting | Jeweler | Repeat client |

### Key journey insights

- **Consultation is the sale.** Most SA studios offer a free first consultation; the design fee (where charged) is credited to the final piece (Prata, ARIAH BELVI pattern).
- **CAD render approval is the risk gate** — once approved, changes cost money; define "2 revisions included" in writing.
- **Heirloom intake** requires an identification/appraisal step before quoting (ZHIVA: "A good redesign starts with a conversation, not a sketch").
- **Remote clients** need WhatsApp video calls + photo/video of renders; this is now standard and expands market beyond Cape Town/JHB.
